About Spice-Compare

Hello! This is spice compare, it's a website that compares the scoville units of different spicy and non-spicy foods. I couldn't find any other website that did something similar that compares foods with different scoville units.

Scoville Units??

Scoville heat units (SHU) was developed by pharmacist Wilbur Scoville to measure how spicy chile peppers are. It works by repersenting how many times the substance must be diluted in sugar water before the spiciness is no longer detectable by taste testers. So if a pepper has 5,000 SHU, that pepper must be diluted 5,000 times before you can no longer tell that it's spicy.
